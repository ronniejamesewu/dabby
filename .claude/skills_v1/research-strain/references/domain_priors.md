## Notation & breeding structures

- **Seed parent (mother) first, pollen parent (father) second** in cross
  notation. A×B and B×A are *reciprocal crosses* — different breeding
  events (organelle genomes ride the mother; growers treat the seed
  parent as setting structure/vigor). Two menu entries differing only in
  order can be deliberate precision, not a typo. [training; applied to
  Erva's Rainbow Colonel/Rainbow Chem]
- **F1/F2**: variability explodes in F2 — an F2 on a menu is a warning
  label. **S1**: a plant selfed via chemical reversal; "X S1" seeds are a
  lottery around X, not X. **BX**: backcross toward a prized cut. **#N**:
  selection N from a seed population (pheno). [training]
- **Clone-only**: the strain IS one physical plant; anything seed-grown
  under its name is a different plant wearing it. A name legitimately
  circulates as the real cut, fake cuts, and seed remakes simultaneously —
  a bigger driver of contradictory scene info than lying is. [training]
- **Producer notation** (In House's storefront/menus, verified): `×` =
  genetic cross, `+` = co-press blend of separately washed cultivars,
  "Pheno Wash"/"Mix" = multi-pheno wash. Check per producer — do not
  assume conventions transfer between brands. [verified]

## Hash-specific

- **Washer genetics**: hash-first breeders select for trichome gland
  morphology and yield ("dumps"). Famous washer families: Z/Zkittlez
  lines, Papaya, GMO, grape lines. A menu dense in these signals a
  genetics-literate solventless operation. [training; the In House/Bloom
  catalog is empirically Z-saturated — verified]
- **Quality is conjunctive**: fire rosin requires excellent buds AND
  excellent wash/sieve/press technique; at the jar the factors are
  indistinguishable, so the quality signal certifies the pipeline
  (grower + processor jointly). [practitioner]
- **CO regulatory lanes** (verified Aug 2026, practitioner check pending):
  licensed shelf flower — assume rad-treated (economics); licensed live
  rosin — fresh-frozen input structurally skips the flower treatment
  lane, gate moves to finished-concentrate testing; trad market — never
  treated, reputation-policed. See research/brands.md "Channel facts".

## Chemistry for the interest read

- **Terpene inheritance segregates, it doesn't average** — synthase genes
  make a cross lean hard toward one parent's profile; pheno variance is
  the norm (why pheno hunts exist). Interest reads talk in betting lines.
  [training]
- **GMO/Chem-family "garlic/gas" character is substantially volatile
  sulfur compounds (VSCs)**, a separate chemical class from the terpene
  reference — expect it to survive temperature differently than fruit
  terps. [training]
- Terpene profiles are inferred from genetics, never measured, per the
  project's epistemic flags — and the lineage itself is an attributed
  claim, so the inference is two hedges deep. Say so.

## Market epistemics

- Renames and invented lineages are common (best case a pheno hunt, worst
  case a whim); contradictory scene info is often evidence of the
  marketing layer at work. Record claims with claimant+date; let the
  jar log adjudicate producers over time. [practitioner]
- Output quality is itself evidence about documentation quality — artisan
  cultivation requires control and records, so demonstrated product
  quality raises a producer's claim prior above the market base rate.
  [practitioner]
- Trust statements, scoped correctly: believe what a brand states
  (weighted by earned trust); a brand-level claim (e.g. "single source")
  cannot pin an individual jar once the brand demonstrably runs multiple
  sources. Never manufacture a statement nobody made. [practitioner]
