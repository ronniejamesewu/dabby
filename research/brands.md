## Colorado ecosystem (research targets — none acquired as of Aug 30, 2026)

### Erva (@ervacolorado)
Grower + breeder-adjacent (works with TGR/Terp Fountain lines). Licensed
CO cultivation only — no MIP license, cannot process own flower (structural
reason for the In House collab, stated by Eric Aug 2026). Stellar
cultivation rep (user-direct). Publishes lineage-rich IG drop menus —
anchor surface. "Erva" = "herb" in Portuguese (their own menu footer).
Reciprocal-cross-level precision on menus (Rainbow Colonel vs Rainbow Chem).
**Stated breeder roster** (Erva's own credit comment, 8.7.26 drop post,
anchor): Three's Genetic Reserve / @jlsmonster, Fruitfull Seeds, Elemental
Seeds, Bloom Seed Co, Purple City (Europe handle). Terp Fountain absent
from that comment but corroborated separately via Red Pebbles.

### In House / In House Melts (@inhousemelts, theinhousellc.com)
Processor + grower. CO licensed (403R-00250), family-owned. Historically
single-source (grows Bloom Seed Co genetics in own Denver facility —
stated on their site); now also processes other grows (Erva collab, July
2026) — so brand-level "single source" no longer pins any individual jar:
per-jar provenance needs per-jar evidence. Weedmaps storefront carries "="
lineage formulas (anchor surface). Product line is heavily blends (`+`)
and pheno washes. Distinct from "In House Genetics" (WA) — see SOURCES.md.

### Bloom Seed Co (bloomseed.co, @bloomseedco)
Breeder. In House's primary genetics partner ("growing, pheno-hunting,
and processing Bloom Seed Co.'s latest" — In House site, anchor).
356-cultivar published catalog, product pages with lineage-tree widgets.
Z-saturated stable (TMZ, Candy Fumez, World War Z...). Deliberately does
not disclose some foundational studs (Orange Mints). Historical note:
Tropicana Cookies was originally bred by Harry Palms *for* Bloom.

### Three's Genetic Reserve / TGR (threesgeneticreserve.com, @jlsmonster = Jeremy Staton, "Seed Maker @threesgeneticreserve")
Breeder behind Erva's line (Uncle Lazer, Rainbow Colonel, Crashglow,
Colonel Chem families; credited in Erva's IG comments). Small catalog
(~30 SKUs); parentage of key studs (Crashglow, Colonel Chem) unpublished.
IG private — follow = best open research lead.

### Terp Fountain Genetics (@terpfountaingenetics)
Breeder. Bio: "Home of the Piegasm, Berry Fizz, FPOZ, Watermelon Pixie."
Likely source of Erva's Red Pebbles cross. Michigan-associated with CO
hash history. No official catalog site found; retail via Terpy Seeds.
IG private.

## Channel facts (Colorado, verified Aug 2026)

- Licensed shelf **flower**: assume rad-treated (market-structure economics,
  Eric-stated; the mandate is passing microbial tests — treatment is the de
  facto route at scale, remediation-after-failure is the legal minimum).
- Licensed **live rosin**: fresh-frozen input structurally skips the
  flower-stage treatment lane; compliance gate moves to microbial +
  mycotoxin testing of the finished concentrate ("concentrates made from
  wet whole plants that were not tested for microbials" is an explicit
  rule category). Treatment-tax folklore largely moot for live rosin.
  Pending practitioner check via Eric's industry friends.
- Traditional market: never treated; bio status untested; cold chain
  unobservable; reputation is the enforcement layer. Cold-chain risk is
  actually universal — the licensed market's edge is only that the last
  link (dispensary storage) is auditable in person.
