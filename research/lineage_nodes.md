# Lineage Nodes — Shared Ancestry Forest

Single source of truth for cultivars that appear inside multiple strain
entries. Each node: cross (with evidence word + claimant), termination
status. Classics (see the research-strain skill's stop-list) get no node —
they are terminal by definition. Campaign date for all nodes: Aug 29–30,
2026, unless a line says otherwise.

## Bloom Seed Co orbit

- **TMZ (Too Much Zkittlez)** = Zkittlez × Candy Fumez — stated (Bloom
  catalog + Perle di Sole tree widget). Terminates via Candy Fumez.
- **Candy Fumez** = Zkittlez × Sherbanger — stated (Bloom); corroborated
  (Boston Roots breeder interview, Honeysuckle Magazine). Terminates.
- **Sherbanger** = Sunset Sherbert × Headbanger — corroborated (Boston
  Roots Seed Co, John of Boston; multiple sources). Both classics. Not a
  Bloom breed — acquired stud.
- **Too Much Lime** = Lime Heads × TMZ — stated (Bloom).
- **Lime Heads** = Lime 1 (clone) × Mandelbrot 707 Headband — stated
  (co-breeder Skunktek's own page; "Meangene x Skunktek"). Grandparents
  are Emerald Triangle clone-only heirloom stock — dead-end beyond.
- **Orange Mints** — DEAD-END, deliberate: parent in 20+ Bloom crosses;
  Bloom's own lineage widget renders it as an unexpandable leaf. Seed
  Junky's same-named "Orange Mints" (Orange Push Pop × Animal Mints) is a
  different cultivar — do not merge. IG lead: Bloom credited Perle di Sole
  to hash hand "bobrosin".
- **Black Maple** = Dulce de Uva × Sherbanger — stated (Bloom).
- **Dulce de Uva** = Ice Cream Cake × (Grape Pie × Wedding Crasher) —
  stated (Bloom). All classics.
- **Rainbow Guava** = Rainbow Belts × Strawberry Guava — stated (Bloom).
- **Strawberry Guava** = Strawberry Banana × Papaya — stated (Bloom).
- **Guava Push Pop** = Rainbow Guava 5 × Orange Mints — stated (Bloom,
  Active tier).
- **Guava'z** (phenos #74/#62) — distinct from Pure Guava; Bloom discloses
  no cross; SeedFinder's algorithmic guess treated as noise. Undisclosed.
- **Rambutan** = Papaya × Guava'z — stated (Bloom). In House sells phenos
  #250/#357 (numbering unconfirmed externally).
- **Pomelo Punch** = Orange Mints × Papaya — stated (Bloom, Proven, Aug
  2023).
- **World War Z** = Zkittlez × TMZ — stated (Bloom). Full chain closes at
  classics; Zkittlez appears at three consecutive generations. NO
  documented link to Quasi Farms' "WW Z" (checked; different state/producer).
- **Papaya** — 710 Labs states "Mystery / Clone Only" (dead-end, anchor).
  Clone-history claim Citral #13 × Ice #2 (Nirvana Seeds) — corroborated,
  never producer-confirmed; one outlier says Mango × Citral #13
  (conflicted). Bloom also lists Papaya with no cross.
- **The Hive** = Honey Banana × Papaya — stated (Bloom). NOT established
  as related to In House's "Gorilla Hive" (shared word only).

## In House orbit (non-Bloom parents)

- **Honey Banana** = Strawberry Banana × Honey Boo Boo — stated (DNA
  Genetics' own site; overrules Elemental-Seeds and Honey Boo Boo ×
  Banana OG mis-attributions). Honey Boo Boo = Bubba Kush × Captain
  Krypt OG (Captain Krypt OG thinly documented — exhausted).
- **Pinesoul** = Green Bodhi's pheno of Goji OG (Nepali OG × Snow Lotus,
  Bodhi Seeds) — corroborated; a pheno selection, not a cross. Lestoil
  (Pinesoul × Pomelo Punch) is bred by Rocky Mountain High 719
  (corroborated via Strainly listing; RMH719's own site content-thin).
- **Znickerdoodle** = Zkittlez × Snickerdoodle — stated (In House
  Weedmaps storefront). **Snickerdoodle** breeder CONFLICTED: dominant
  candidate Symbiotic (Cookies N Cream × Purple Punch F2, corroborated
  ×3); weaker Sunwest (Cookies N Cream × Juicy Fruit, single source).
  Cookies N Cream = Starfighter × GSC (Exotic Genetix, corroborated).
- **Chunky Diesel** — only documented cross: Deep Chunk × Sour Diesel
  (Ganja Rebel Seed Co for Cresco Labs; corroborated ×6) — but that's an
  IL/multi-state cultivar with NO found CO/In House connection: possible
  name-alike, conflicted-adjacent. Flag when reasoning from it.
- **Sour Banana** = Strawberry Banana Cream × AJ's Cut Sour Diesel (The
  Plug Seedbank; corroborated). Parent-identity wrinkle: Karma's own
  "Strawbanana Cream LTD" (DNA's Strawberry Banana × White OG V2.0) may
  or may not be the same product — conflicted, unresolved.
- **Banana Beach** = GG4 × Sour Banana — stated (In House storefront).
- **Sudachi** = Lime Heads #1 × Sour Diesel IBL — stated (In House
  storefront); matches Bloom's "Sudachi Lime" (Lime Heads × Sour D).
- **Grape Stomper** = Purple Elephant (Purple Urkle × Hashplant) ×
  Chemdog Sour Diesel — near-anchor (Gage Green's own description via
  SeedFinder; JojoRizo's clone). "OG BX" designation: BX male conflicted

  is an unrelated product — do not merge.
- **GMO Rootbeer** = GMO Cookies × Root Beer — corroborated (Freeborn
  Selections, Mean Gene + Skunktek). Root Beer = clone-only/select male,
  dead-end-adjacent.
- **Death Coast** = Death Star × East Coast Sour Diesel — stated (In House
  IG menu 7/31/26). Appears in Bloom's catalog only as a parent (Death
  Guava) — consistent with an In House-side cross.
- **Death Star** = Sensi Star × Sour Diesel — corroborated ×5 (Team Death
  Star, Ohio, ~2001). Both classics.
- **Key Lime Guava** = Rainbow Guava #5 × TMZ — stated (IG menu 7/31/26).
- **Planet Z** = Zkittles × Gelato 33 — stated (IG menu 7/3/26).
- **Fizzy Cola** = Grape Stomper OG BX × GMO Rootbeer — stated (IG menu
  7/3/26).
- **Colada Lime** = Pomelo Punch × Too Much Lime — stated (Bloom + IG menu).
- **Tally Mon** = Papaya × (Banana OG × Do-Si-Dos) — stated (IG menu
  7/3/26). Banana OG itself conflicted: SeedFinder genealogy says Ghost
  OG × Sagarmatha 60/40 banana pheno; received wisdom says OG Kush ×
  Banana; breeder unknown — either way terminates at OG-family classics.
- **Lemon Lime Sour** = Sour Diesel × World War Z — stated (IG menu 7/3/26).
- **Crunch Berries** = Blueberry × Triple OG — stated (IG menu 7/3/26).
  Both classics.
- **Cotton C@ndy Grapes** — UNDISCLOSED and possibly an In House private

  "Cotton Candy Grapes" is a different breeder's unrelated strain. Parents
  Grape Koolada, Sugar Spin #46, Grape Spree #76 all cite it. Top IG-pass
