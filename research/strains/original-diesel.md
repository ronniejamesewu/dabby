## Name-collision map (the referent pool, none ONI-attributed)
All three documented "Original Diesel" seed entries resolve to the same clone-only cut (aka Headband / Daywrecker — not 707 Headband):
- Original Diesel (Unknown or Legendary) → Chemdawg × (Mass Super Skunk × Sensi NL) — corroborated (SeedFinder, Sept 2 2026) → Chem D / Skunk / Afghani-NL classics — terminates.
- Original Diesel (Canadian Seed Lab) → ChemDawg × Sensi NL — corroborated (SeedFinder); presented as a seed release of the same cut.
- Original Diesel (Dr. Underground) S1 → Original Diesel [Daywrecker cut] selfed — corroborated (SeedFinder).
