# Pure Guava

- **Grower:** likely In House (cut lives in Bloom's working library — appears as a parent across their catalog — and In House grows Bloom genetics; assumed) · **Processor:** In House (stated)
- **Type:** single cultivar (cross); "#2" pheno seen in one corroborating reel
- **Cross:** Chunky Diesel × Great White Shark — stated (In House Weedmaps storefront, both SKU formats) and stated (IG menu 7/31/26); corroborated (independent IG reel, parents reversed)

## Chain to classics
- Great White Shark → Super Skunk × White Widow (stated, Green House Seed Co's own page) — terminates
- Chunky Diesel → only documented cross is Deep Chunk × Sour Diesel (Ganja Rebel/Cresco, corroborated ×6) — but no CO/In House connection found: possible name-alike, flagged. See [lineage_nodes.md](../lineage_nodes.md)

## Notes
- Not Guava Gelato-family.
- Parent across Bloom's guava line (Cranberry/Death/Fire Guava etc.); its own parentage never appears in Bloom's catalog.
