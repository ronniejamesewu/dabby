# Point Break

- **Grower:** ONI (brand-stated in-house cultivation, retailer-distributed boilerplate; the Colorado operating licensee is undisclosed — see [brands.md](../brands.md)) · **Processor:** ONI (same statement; cold-cured hash rosin)
- **Type:** single cultivar (cross) — as sold: "REC - Oni - Point Break / Cold Cured Hash Rosin", 2g, Reefer Madness (Dutchie menu, Sept 2 2026)
- **Cross:** undisclosed by ONI for this jar. The circulating Colorado cultivar of this name is **Tropicanna Cookies MTN Cut × Trophy Wife** — stated by Surfr Seeds (surfrseeds.surf/products/point-break, read Sept 2 2026). No ONI-attributed source states that ONI's jar is the Surfr cultivar.
- **Breeder:** Surfr Seeds (stated, own storefront) for the circulating cultivar; ONI's own 43-strain seed catalog (SeedFinder breeder page, read Sept 2 2026) does not list Point Break.

## Chain to classics (conditional on the Surfr claim)
- Tropicanna Cookies → GSC (Forum) × Tangie (corroborated; Harry Palms for Bloom, refined by Oni as F2) — terminates. The Tropicanna parent is credited to Oni Seed Co on SeedFinder's Point Break entry — the one documented genetic thread from this name into Oni's catalog.
- Trophy Wife (Surfr) → Triangle Mints × Triangle Mints F2 → Triangle Kush / Animal Mints → GSC, OG Kush, Blueberry, Hindu Kush landrace — terminates. See [lineage_nodes.md](../lineage_nodes.md).

## Notes
- Westword (June 1 2023) placed this cultivar on ~30 Colorado shelves including Reefer Madness, via Good Chemistry (flower), Green Dot Labs, Mile High Xtractions, Killa Beez — circulating well before any ONI SKU. "Surfr Select" appears as a rosin brand name for it (thehighestcritic.com title; lead, not opened).
- Name collision: other breeders (e.g. Karma Genetics) sell unrelated "Point Break" cultivars — not merged.
- Inference (conditional on the Surfr claim): Tangie/GSC lean on the Tropicanna side. Not measured.

## Open questions
- Is ONI's Point Break the Surfr Seeds cultivar, or a separate ONI selection under a shared name? No party has stated either.
