- **Fresh-press storage — portion-and-freeze** *(July 11, 2026)*. Fresh-press rosin holds its terpene payload and its sappy texture best **frozen** — cold slows terpene off-gassing (Arrhenius: cannabinoid degradation rate roughly doubles per 10°C in a peer-reviewed resin study; volatile monoterpenes should track at least as steeply), and freezing stalls the sauce→badder nucleation that fridge/room temp lets run. The goal is to vape it **cold from frozen** to catch the full terp load, not to cold-cure it.
  - **Action:** portion the bulk into small single-/few-session amounts and freeze *those*, so the whole stash is never thermal-cycled; keep one small working portion in the fridge for hassle-free access. A pump-vac canister on the frozen portions handles oxidation but **not** thermal cycling or condensation — portioning is what handles those.
  - **Condensation:** opening a cold jar pulls humid air onto a cold surface → water in the concentrate (dab "crackle," slow quality loss). Standard advice is warm-it-sealed-15–30-min before opening, which directly conflicts with dab-it-cold — accept fast-grab-and-reseal as the cost; portioning minimizes it by keeping opens off the bulk.
  - **Reusing the empty glass jars (for the portions):** clean with **unscented** dish soap + hot water, hard rinse, then a final distilled/RO rinse to kill soap film and mineral spots. Old stuck rosin: ISO to cut it (99% flashes cleanest) — but that's a *cutting* step only, always followed by a soap wash + rinse; never the last thing to touch the glass. **Dry bone-dry** before filling (air-dry inverted or oven ~225°F/20 min) — residual moisture + frozen storage is the condensation/mold failure this whole plan is trying to avoid. De-labeling is step one of cleaning, not a separate job: a hot-water soak lifts most labels leaving no residue of its own; if adhesive remains, acetone or ISO (glass, so acetone is fine) — but any solvent used is just the first contaminant the clean-out pass then removes.
  - **Honesty flags:** the "~90% retained at fridge over 12 mo" retention numbers are industry-aggregator lore, not peer-reviewed; fridge→freezer is a *marginal* gain on top of the big room→cold win; whether cold-cure's louder nose is net terpene loss vs. structural redistribution vs. transformation is genuinely unresolved (no GC-MS in the surfaced sources); and the jar-cleaning guidance is all extractor/vendor blogs — the chemistry is sound and checkable, but no specific number is lab-verified. Researched this session via two background agents (terpene/storage physics; jar cleaning + de-labeling). Related open item: the "cold-cure continues in fridge — nose improves" wisdom row reads that nose-gain as a positive and may deserve a caveat given the unresolved-mechanism finding (session-close call).
